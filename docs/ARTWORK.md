# MovieMagic artwork

The built-in image generation tool produced the original fictional imagery. Final assets are in `static/images/`.

- `hero.png` came from a wide photorealistic action-film still brief. A lone driver stands beside a charcoal car in a rainy neon-lit Mumbai street at midnight, with crimson reflections on the right and atmospheric shadows on the left. The artwork contains no titles, marks or logos.
- `poster-1.png` through `poster-14.png` came from an equal-cell 4-by-4 portrait contact sheet brief. The scenes include a neon driver, a monsoon silhouette, a couple at a cinema, a radio operator, rival chefs, a coastal fisher, a parkour runner, a groom, a railway romance, a ghost platform, a courtyard family, a wildfire rescue, an indie guitarist and a comedy microphone. The first 14 cells were cropped as separate poster assets.
